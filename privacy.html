Privacy Policy

What this site handles about you, why, who else is involved, and how to have it removed.

Last updated 17 October 2026

The short version

You can browse the leaderboards, raffles and store without telling us anything. If you log in with Kick, we keep your Kick username and profile picture so the site knows who you are. If you use the Points Store, link a casino username for raffles, or send us a message, we keep what that needs. We sell nothing to anybody, and we run no advertising trackers.

If you just browse

  • Your leaderboard choice. When you pick Razed or Razed.IO, your browser saves that choice in its local storage (a single entry named rekoj-board), so the same board opens next time. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • The copy button. The "CODE: REKOJ" button puts the code on your clipboard. The site never reads your clipboard.
  • Technical data. Like any website, the server that hosts this site sees the IP address and browser details of each request. That is used to deliver the pages, prevent abuse and keep the site running. It is not built into a profile of you.
  • Fonts. Pages load the Rubik typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser requests it from Google's servers, and Google receives your IP address and browser details.

If you log in with Kick

Logging in sends you to Kick, which asks whether to share your basic profile with RekoJ (the user:read permission). We receive and keep your Kick user ID, username and profile picture, and when you first and last logged in. We never see your Kick password, email address or anything else on your Kick account.

Rekoj points and the Points Store

Rekoj points are your loyalty points on RekoJ's Kick channel, run by BotRix. To show your balance and hours watched, and to take or refund points when you order something, the site sends your Kick username to BotRix. When you order an item we keep the order: what it was, its price in points, when you ordered, its status, and any note from an admin about delivering it. Admin point adjustments are logged with the username, the amount and the reason.

Wager raffles and linked casino accounts

To enter raffles you can link the username you play under at Razed or Razed.IO. We keep the username you enter, whether an admin approved, rejected or removed it, and any note they left. To work out raffle tickets, the site reads from the casino's affiliate reporting how much that username wagered under the code REKOJ during each raffle's window. When you join a raffle we keep your entry; when it is drawn we keep each entry's wager and tickets so the result can be checked later. Admin actions on raffles (creating, editing, removing an entry, drawing) are recorded in an activity log.

If you send us a message

The contact form asks for a message and a way to reply (a Kick or Discord username, or an email address), and optionally your name. We keep the message, the reply details you give, when it was sent, and whether we've dealt with it. If you're logged in we also note your Kick username. Messages are read in the RekoJ admin panel, and may also be forwarded to a private RekoJ Discord channel so the team sees them quickly.

Analytics

The site can use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are used. It is only switched on if RekoJ has configured it, and even then it only loads after you accept it in the banner shown on your first visit. If you decline, or never answer, no analytics code runs and no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is saved in your browser (an entry named rekoj-consent), and you can change it at any time with the link.

What is shown publicly

  • Leaderboards show players' usernames (shortened and partly hidden, for example "Admo***"), amounts wagered and prizes, for players who signed up at Razed or Razed.IO under the code REKOJ. These figures come from the casinos' affiliate reporting and appear whether or not a player has ever visited this site. The pictures beside them are the site's own artwork, not players' avatars.
  • Raffle winners are shown by Kick username under Past Raffles and may be shown on stream.
  • Store orders may appear on stream as an alert with your Kick username, profile picture and the item.

Cookies

  • rekoj_session keeps you logged in after you log in with Kick. It is signed so it can't be forged, is not readable by page scripts, and lasts 30 days or until you log out.
  • rekoj_oauth exists only for the few minutes it takes to log in with Kick, to protect the sign-in against forgery. It is deleted when sign-in finishes.
  • Google Analytics cookies (named _ga and _ga_…) are set only if analytics is configured and you accept it.

The first two are needed for logging in to work. The site sets no advertising or social tracking cookies.

Who else is involved

  • Kick handles logging in, under its own privacy policy.
  • BotRix runs the channel points and receives your Kick username when the site checks or changes your points.
  • Razed and Razed.IO provide the affiliate figures the leaderboards and raffle tickets are built from. We read figures from them; we don't send them anything about you.
  • Vercel hosts the site, and Neon hosts the database where accounts, links, orders, raffles and messages are kept.
  • Google serves the fonts and, only if you accept it, analytics.
  • Discord may receive contact messages if forwarding to the team's channel is switched on.
  • Kick, Discord and X links. Social links and "Visit" buttons take you to someone else's site, under their privacy policy, not this one.

Nobody else receives anything, and none of it is sold, rented or used for advertising.

How long it is kept

  • Your account, linked casino usernames, orders and raffle entries are kept while the site runs, so orders can be delivered and raffle results checked, until you ask us to delete them.
  • Activity logs keep the most recent 1,000 to 2,000 entries; older entries drop off.
  • Contact messages are kept until we've dealt with them and no longer need them.
  • Server logs are kept only as long as our hosting provider needs them to run and protect the site.

Your choices

  • Log out at any time from the account menu or your profile; this deletes the session cookie.
  • See or delete your data. Ask through the contact form and we'll tell you what we hold about you, or delete it. Deleting your account also removes your linked usernames and raffle entries; orders we still owe you are settled first.
  • Hide your name from a leaderboard. Ask us and we'll hide your username from the public board. Your standing, and any prize you win, is not affected.
  • Your casino account is separate. Anything held by Razed or Razed.IO is theirs to manage, under their own policies.

Under 18s

This site is for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18, or under the legal gambling age where they live if that is higher. Please don't log in or link an account if that's you.

Changes to this policy

If what the site handles changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated.
