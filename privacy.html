Privacy Policy

What this site handles about you, why, and how to have it removed. The list is short, and this page covers all of it.

Last updated 22 September 2026

The short version

RekoJ has no accounts, no sign-in and no forms. You can use every part of the site without telling us anything. We don't ask for your name, email or address. We run no analytics or advertising trackers, and we sell nothing to anybody.

What the site handles

  • Your leaderboard choice. When you pick Razed or Razed.IO, your browser saves that choice in its local storage (a single entry named rekoj-board), so the same board opens next time. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
  • The copy button. The "CODE: REKOJ" button puts the code on your clipboard. The site never reads your clipboard.
  • Technical data. Like any website, the server that hosts this site sees the IP address and browser details of each request. That is used to deliver the pages and keep the site running, and it is not built into a profile of you.
  • Fonts. The page loads the Rubik typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser requests it from Google's servers, and Google receives your IP address and browser details in the process. Google describes how it handles this in its own privacy policy.

What is shown publicly

The leaderboards show players' usernames (shortened and partly hidden, for example "Admo***"), avatars, amounts wagered and prizes for players who signed up at Razed or Razed.IO under the code REKOJ. These figures come from the casinos' affiliate reporting. That's the point of a public board, and it applies whether or not a player has ever visited this site. Nothing else about any player is shown.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own: no sign-in cookies, no analytics cookies, and no advertising or social tracking cookies. The one thing it stores is the leaderboard choice described above, and that stays in your browser.

Who else is involved

  • Razed and Razed.IO provide the affiliate figures the boards are built from. We read figures from them; we don't send them anything about you.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface, as described above.
  • Our hosting provider runs the server the site is delivered from and handles requests on our behalf.
  • Kick, Discord and X. The social links take you to those platforms. Once you follow a link, or any "Visit" button to a casino, you are on someone else's site and under their privacy policy, not this one.

Nobody else receives anything, and none of it is sold, rented or used for advertising.

How long it is kept

Leaderboard standings are kept for the month they belong to, so results can be checked after it closes. Server logs are kept only as long as our hosting provider needs them to run and protect the site. Your saved leaderboard choice stays on your device until you clear it.

Your choices

  • Removing your name from the board. If you appear on a leaderboard and would rather not, message us and we will hide your username from the public board. Your standing, and any prize you win, is not affected.
  • Asking what we hold. Message us and we will tell you what, if anything, we hold about you, or delete it.
  • Your casino account is a separate matter. Anything held by Razed or Razed.IO is theirs to manage, under their own policies.
